Severity: **H** changes a headline conclusion's *meaning*, **N** changes a number, **C** cosmetic/latent.

"PASS in all 151 games" for `research_unvalidated` was therefore mechanical, not evidence. Old tests passed only because they used crossed quotes (ask 0.40 vs mid 0.60). | **Fixed** (`(w·d)²` removed; tests with realistic books). ## Net assessment of PR #52

The data layer, taxonomy, resolver and the central negative finding are sound and reproducible. The two things that materially overstated what the audit proved were (1) the expression-engine "PASS everywhere" (mechanically impossible to violate) and (2) the PROMISING / REJECTED labels and the per-run prospective read. All four are corrected in separate, reviewable commits. **The audit's conclusion — no demonstrated executable edge — stands**, and is now somewhat stronger (the corrected counterfactual engine loses 16.5%). It remains a finding about 151 games and a fee-heavy market, not a proof that no edge exists.
