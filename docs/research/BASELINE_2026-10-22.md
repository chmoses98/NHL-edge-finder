## 3. What V1 predicts well, and poorly (9,781 games, 2018–2025, see `oa-walk-forward/RESULTS.md`)

* **Well:** who wins and by how much — beats a league-average model by 0.0064–0.0095 Brier on win probability and 0.03–0.04 goals of RPS on margin; the mean total is calibrated (6.16 predicted vs 6.16 realised); the total-goal width is calibrated-to-slightly-wide (z² 0.91); calibration of win probability is reasonable (ECE 0.028).
* **Poorly:** overtime (17.5% predicted vs 22.0% realised, every season), large margins (|margin| ≥ 3: 34.6% vs 39.9%), total-goal *differentiation* (no better than league average in 2024–25), shrinkage that is too strong (a re-tuned V1 gains 0.0018 Brier), and it trails the market on every family.
* **Not a defect after all:** "goal distributions too narrow" (rejected); "goalie status over-confident" (rejected earlier: CONFIRMED 97.8% vs stated 98.5%).

## 4. Where historical evidence is insufficient

| area | limitation |
|---|---|
| goalies | no point-in-time starter history; all historical results have the goalie term off or use the *actual* starter (oracle). Live confirmation capture lags the source by a median 27.6 min (pre-PR #59 hourly refresh). |
| lineups, injuries, PP units | no historical timestamps; player results are conditional on the actual dressed lineup |
| market | Kalshi history only for 2025-26, midpoints without depth; sportsbook consensus only for 147 live games; Kalshi minute-level reaction to goalie news not resolvable |
| vendor data | MoneyPuck xG for past games is as published today (retroactively refit) |
| sample | NHL outcomes are very noisy: ~1,300 games per season resolve only ≈ 0.002 Brier; seasons cannot be judged one at a time |
| selection | the 2024–25 seasons were inspected by earlier work, so they are "exposed"; only 2018–2023 are unexposed for the opponent-adjustment study; 2026-27 after 2026-10-21T17:13:05Z is untouched |
