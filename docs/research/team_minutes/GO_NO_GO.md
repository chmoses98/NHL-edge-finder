## Risks and limits (stated plainly)
* **Evidence level.** 2022-2025 is development evidence; the independent check is 17 games so far (direction confirmed, intervals wide on PP/SH/shots). The soak above is the validation; the risk of activating earlier is a research arm that is a few percent off in either direction, reversible in one step.
* **Shots remain under-predicted** by the candidate too (-0.03 per skater-game historically, -0.07 on current games). Minutes explain about a tenth of that; the shot-rate level is the LC track. This change should not be sold as a shots fix.
* **Data artefact found.** The recorded team-state seconds of overtime games are padded to an 80:00 grid. It deflates recorded EN/EV shares in those games (absorbed by `kappa`) and made my Sprint 6 league-minutes table wrong for EA/EN/total (erratum added). Fixing the padding at the source would change shares and therefore goals/assists/points; it is deliberately **not** part of this change and would need its own evaluation.
* **Parameter drift.** `pp_per_ppmin` was 1.10 in every training season (range 1.097-1.104 across the fits), but league PP levels move (5.45 min in 2022, 4.80 in 2024). V2's `ppmin` tracks the season, so the allocation follows; refit at season's end, never automatically.
* **Live lineups are projected, the fit used actual lineups.** On the 34 team-sides of the current-games replay (`LINES_PROJECTED`, 17.8 projected skaters per team vs 18 dressed in the fit) the dressed skaters' shares sum to EV 4.85 / PP 4.78 / SH 3.86 (fit: 4.89 / 4.95 / 4.00), i.e. 1-4% lower, so live team totals will sit slightly under the skater-minute budget. The per-player bias on the 17 games is still ~0 (total -0.03 min), because players who are projected but do not play are excluded from scoring, but the diagnostic is recorded in every shadow row (`share_*`) and should be watched during the soak.
* **Mixed archives.** After activation, analyses that read `predictions_player` across the switch must use `meta_team_minutes_version` / `*_legacy` (the player-minutes comparators already do).
* **Not covered:** playoff games (the fit is regular-season), neutral-site/outdoor games without V2 special-teams components (they take the league-clock fallback), and any change to who plays (lineups are unchanged).
